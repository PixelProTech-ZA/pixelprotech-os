ABOUT & PRIVACY

Pixel Office is a free documents, spreadsheet, slides and invoice tool from PixelProTech Solutions. It is provided as is, free of charge.

Your data stays on this computer. Everything you type is saved only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account, tracking or analytics.

Shared computer? Anyone using this browser on this computer can open what was saved. Download your work (Docs, Sheets and Invoice have download or print options), then clear it before the next learner.
