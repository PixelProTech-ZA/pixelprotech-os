// SECTION 04 — SPACE PSYCHOLOGY

SPACE
PSYCHOLOGY

What the listener feels is determined by where you place them.

🔬
SHORT REVERB · PLATE / ROOM
INTIMACY
"You're close to the source. The room is small. You feel the breath."
Use: 0.3s–0.8s decay · Small room or plate reverb · Pre-delay 10–20ms

Effect: The listener perceives proximity. Great for lead vocals, spoken word, intimate piano, and close-mic'd acoustics. This is the sound of someone in the same room as you.
🏔
LONG REVERB · HALL / CHAMBER
DISTANCE
"The source is far away. The room swallows the sound."
Use: 2s–5s decay · Hall or cathedral · Pre-delay 30–60ms

Effect: Creates epic scale and emotional vastness. Use on pads, orchestral elements, snare room mics, and background vocals. The sound arrives, then echoes into silence. Distance = grandeur.
🎙
NO REVERB · DRY VOCAL
UPFRONT EMOTION
"Every word lands with full weight. Nothing softens the truth."
Use: 0% reverb · Surgical EQ to clean transients · Intimate mic placement

Effect: Dry vocals hit harder. The listener has nowhere to hide from the emotion. Use sparingly for raw moments, spoken lyrics, or confrontational passages. Kendrick. D'Angelo. Facts without filter.
🌫
WET VOCAL · SHIMMER REVERB
DREAMSTATE
"The voice becomes the atmosphere itself."
Use: 30–50% wet · Shimmer / reverse reverb · No pre-delay · High-pass reverb tail

Effect: The vocal becomes diffuse, half-present, like a memory. Used in neo-soul, dream-pop, ambient trap, lo-fi. The voice is felt, not heard. Use only when the lyric is secondary to the vibe.
⏱
TEMPO-SYNCED DELAY
MOVEMENT
"The sound walks through time. One becomes many."
Use: 1/8 or 1/4 note · 20–35% feedback · Ping-pong for width

Effect: Delay creates rhythmic energy. The original signal repeats in time with the groove, filling space without adding new instruments. The mix breathes and moves. Use it as composition, not correction.
🌐
STEREO REVERB + DELAY
ENVIRONMENT
"The mix becomes a place. The listener is inside it."
Use: Wide stereo reverb · Separate delay bus · High-pass reverb tail above 200Hz

Effect: When reverb and delay work together through separate buses, they build a three-dimensional acoustic environment. The listener stops listening to a mix. They're in a room. That room is yours to design.
