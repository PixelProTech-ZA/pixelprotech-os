PIXEL GUIDE - put the AI helper online (run these in this folder, on your computer)

Needs Node.js 22.12 or newer. Check with:  node --version

1) npx wrangler@latest login
2) npx wrangler@latest deploy
3) npx wrangler@latest secret put OPENROUTER_API_KEY
   (paste the key when it asks; nothing shows while you paste; press Enter)

Check it: open  https://pixel-guide-api.lindokuhlerojasmabika.workers.dev/health
It should say  {"ok":true,"configured":true}

If step 2 prints a different address, open Pixel Guide in the OS, tap "Connection",
paste that address and save.

Do not upload this folder or your key to GitHub.
In your OpenRouter account, set a credit limit on this key.
