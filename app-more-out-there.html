There Is More Out There

An underground map of legitimate, overlooked music resources.

Most people think the music world is Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Splice and DistroKid. Frequency Oasis goes fishing beyond the obvious — across the wider internet for legitimate resources that are old, obscure, regional, independent, experimental, niche, or simply unfashionable — and brings what's useful back into the Oasis.

Every resource here ("a Catch") is checked before publication — confirmed real, confirmed still operating, licence terms stated plainly. FO is not a marketplace and doesn't host or resell anyone's music. We document where things live and send you to the real source.

How a Catch gets published

Every resource is checked before publication — confirming it's real, still operating, and that FO can honestly answer what it is, what it costs, what licence applies, and what it's genuinely useful for. If we can't answer those honestly, we don't publish the page.

Licence badges

  • COMMERCIAL USE ALLOWED — the source confirms commercial use under the stated licence.
  • LICENCE VARIES — varies by individual item; check before using anything specific.
  • NON-COMMERCIAL ONLY — the source explicitly restricts use to non-commercial purposes.
  • DISCOVERY ONLY — for listening; don't assume reuse rights.

Status, not permanence

Resources disappear. Links die. Every Catch carries a status (Active / Check / Broken / Archived / Removed) and a "FO checked" date. We don't silently keep dead information alive.

South African roots, global scope

Frequency Oasis is forged in South Africa. We actively look for African and South African music archives, regional radio and independent infrastructure alongside everything else.
