PixelProTech Open Library is a catalogue. It lists selected titles and links to their official source pages, and it hosts no book files. Licence, edition and territorial copyright status must be checked per title before any file is copied, hosted or redistributed. Once opened, the catalogue works offline; reading a book needs a connection to its source.
