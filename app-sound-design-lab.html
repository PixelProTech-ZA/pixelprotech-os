The Origin of Frequency Oasis

We create. We don't copy.

S

Lindokuhle Rojas Mabika

Known professionally as Smokey Eye Beats · Founded 2018 · Durban, South Africa

Frequency Oasis was founded in 2018 by Lindokuhle Rojas Mabika, professionally known as Smokey Eye Beats. The journey began in Durban, South Africa, recording the sounds most people ignored — streets, forests, towns, rain, machinery, birds, and everyday environments.

Back inside the DAW, those recordings were chopped, layered, stretched, and transformed into entirely new instruments and textures. The mission has always remained the same.

"Create the sounds the music industry doesn't know it's missing yet."

Frequency Oasis teaches producers not only how to use sound, but how to hear it differently.
