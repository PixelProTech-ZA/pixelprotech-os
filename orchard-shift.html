How to play

  1. Flip the gates. Tap a gate on the field, or use the big buttons below, to switch which way fruit rolls. The glowing route shows where it goes next.
  2. Match the basket. Each fruit belongs in the basket with its badge. Apple Citrus Plum
  3. Build combos. Correct deliveries in a row raise your multiplier, up to ×5. A wrong basket resets it, and in Challenge costs a strike.
  4. Watch the chips. NEXT shows the fruit coming. Every 12 fruit starts a new SHIFT: a little faster each time.

Keyboard: A gate A · B gate B · Space pause · M sound
